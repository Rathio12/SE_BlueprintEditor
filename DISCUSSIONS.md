## Profile presets

The app ships with presets for vanilla worlds, **Sigma Draconis – Expanse** and a **typical modded server**.
More presets are added from the community:

1. **Request one** in [Ideas](https://github.com/Rathio12/SE_BlueprintEditor/discussions/new?category=ideas) → *Server profile preset*, with a link to where the server publishes its limits.
2. **Or share your own** in [Show and tell](https://github.com/Rathio12/SE_BlueprintEditor/discussions/new?category=show-and-tell):
   LIMIT PROFILES → select the profile → **Export**, then paste the JSON.
3. Others use it with **Import** in the LIMIT PROFILES tab — app or website.

Presets only become built-in when the limits can be checked against the server's own rules.
The profile format is described in the wiki: [Limits and profiles](docs/wiki/Limits-and-Profiles.md).

## Good to know

- Be friendly and on topic — the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) applies here too.
- Mention the **app version** (bottom right of the window) and whether you use the **app or the website**.
- For mods, the **Workshop ID** (the number in the mod's Steam URL) helps more than the name.
