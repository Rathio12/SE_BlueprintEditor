SE Blueprint Inspector — Space Engineers blueprint viewer in your browser

Open a Space Engineers blueprint (bp.sbc or a whole blueprint folder) and see its blocks, PCU, fixed guns, turrets, cargo volume, mass, thrust, power and jump range, plus the full build cost in components, ingots and ore. Check it against vanilla world limits, the Sigma Draconis Expanse limit or your own server profile. Modded blocks and WeaponCore weapons are supported when you add the mod folders.

Everything runs locally in your browser — blueprints and mods are never uploaded. Read the wiki · Download the free Windows app
